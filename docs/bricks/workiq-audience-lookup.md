# Build a real audience profile with Work IQ

**Tool:** Work IQ (usable from Cowork or Scout)
**Source status:** Liftable from Agent Academy — Work IQ

Most audience profiles are guesses dressed as research. Work IQ reads real organisational data you already have access to — job titles, teams, reporting lines, meeting topics, collaboration patterns — and turns it into a profile grounded in what people actually do, not what you assume they do.

## Steps

1. **Start with a role or group you need to understand.** Name the audience: a team, a job family, a set of people who will use what you build. Be specific enough that Work IQ can look them up — a department name, a title pattern, a distribution list.

2. **Pull organisational data.** Ask Work IQ to find people matching that description. It returns what your tenant makes visible to you: titles, departments, managers, who works with whom. This is the same data you would see in the org directory — Work IQ reads it faster.

3. **Look at what they spend time on.** Ask Work IQ about meeting patterns, collaboration frequency, and working relationships **at role level** — what this kind of person's week looks like, not what one named individual's week looks like. A profile built from calendar reality is harder to argue with than one built from assumption.

4. **Identify the dimensions that change your build.** From the data, pull out the things that would change what you make for them: authority level, tool access, time constraints, how they consume information, who they report to. These are your design constraints.

5. **Write the audience profile.** Assemble what you found into a short profile: who they are, what their day looks like, what they can and cannot do, how they prefer to receive information. Ground every claim in something Work IQ showed you.

6. **Cite or cut.** Go through the profile line by line. Every claim should trace to something Work IQ actually showed you. Anything that doesn't, mark as an assumption — or delete it. A profile where you can't tell the evidence from the guesswork is worse than no profile.

::: warning Privacy and respect
Work IQ reads organisational data your account can already access — the same directory, calendar, and collaboration data visible in Microsoft 365. It does not surface anything you could not find yourself.

**What you learn about real colleagues stays in your build.** Do not share individual people's calendar patterns, reporting lines, or collaboration data outside your working session. Use the data to build a better product, not to profile individuals.
:::

## Apply it to your scenario

> Your scenario has a target audience. Instead of inventing a persona, use Work IQ to find real people who match — then build your profile from what the data says about their roles, their time, and their access. The profile becomes the input to whatever you build next: an evaluation, a skill, a recommendation. Ground it in data, not in guesses.
